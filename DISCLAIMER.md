## Numbers are estimates

Every token count, request count, quota percentage, reset countdown and cost shown by Glyphi is
derived from files that other tools write on your computer, or from endpoints those tools expose. The
figures can be incomplete, delayed, rounded or wrong. In particular:

- a provider may change its log format, price list or quota window at any time;
- a local file may be rotated, truncated, locked or deleted before Glyphi reads it;
- cache reads, cache writes, batch discounts, service tiers, taxes and currency conversion may not be
  represented exactly;
- days can be marked incomplete when only part of the activity was available.

The displayed cost is an **estimate**, not a bill. Reconcile it with the invoice your provider issues
before relying on it.
