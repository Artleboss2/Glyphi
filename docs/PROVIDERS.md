# Supported providers

Glyphi ships a directory of **305 AI developer tools**. 20 have a local reader in the engine today; the rest are still detected on your computer, so the app can tell you whether it finds their store before you turn anything on.

A provider is *tracked* when the engine can read its usage from local files. Nothing is enabled without your consent: detection is passive, and the switch in Settings → Providers is the only thing that starts counting.
